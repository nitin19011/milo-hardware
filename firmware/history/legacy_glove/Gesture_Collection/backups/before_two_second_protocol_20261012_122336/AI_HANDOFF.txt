MILO GLOVE DATASET HANDOFF - PERSONAL MODEL FOR NITIN

TASK
Eight labels: come, sit, paw, spin, bang, throw_overhand, throw_underhand, background.
Spin means point then clockwise hand rotation from wearer's view. No roll-over class.
Treat the default bang thumb pose and gesture definitions as a protocol agreement to confirm before final data collection.
Background includes hard negatives: fist alone, a swing with no release, already-open arm movements.
Voice matching/Milo action execution is downstream; this package collects glove gesture data only.

CANONICAL RECORDS
Per-clip CSV retains actual ESP timestamps, sequence numbers, raw flex ADC, SI IMU values,
raw IMU counts, true IMU validity, rail mask, drop/error counters and RSSI.
Per-clip JSON carries class, repetition, variant, speed prompt, quality result, human accept,
cue timing, release candidate and optional human event boundaries.
session.json identifies participant, wear session, split role and gesture-definition hash.
calibration.json holds open, fist, loose-grip medians and the stationary gyro bias for that session.
Raw_stream.jsonl preserves all attempts, idle data and delayed arrivals.

PRIMARY FEATURE ORDER (9 CHANNELS)
ax, ay, az: m/s^2 (includes gravity; no unrequested orientation/sensor-fusion transform).
gx, gy, gz: rad/s.
thumb, index, middle: raw averaged 12-bit ADC counts.
Hardware: MPU-6050 +/-16 g, +/-2000 deg/s, 21 Hz filter; 50 Hz host sampling.
Flex: eight ADC readings averaged after dummy read/200 us settling, GPIO35/34/39.
Do not feed class labels, trial numbers, session IDs, RSSI or acquisition sequence numbers as model features.

TENSOR EXPORTS
dataset.npz: load using numpy.load(..., allow_pickle=False).
X_raw: float32 [N,300,9], full 6-second clips, physical IMU units + raw flex ADC.
X_session_calibrated: [N,300,9], same axes/order, subtract session gyro bias, normalise flex per wear session.
X_raw_channels_first / X_calibrated_channels_first: [N,9,300] for Conv1d/time-series tooling.
X_action_raw / X_action_calibrated: [N,150,9], the cue-aligned 1-4-second view.
y: int64 [N]. classes[y[i]] is the label; labels also retains strings.
feature_names, session_ids, clip_ids, split_roles and train/validation/test indices are included.
Independent .npy files are provided for X_raw, X_session_calibrated, y, labels and session_ids.
all_samples.csv is long format with clip/class/participant/session/split identifiers.
clips.jsonl contains one complete raw/calibrated series plus metadata per line.
dataset.ts uses the sktime/UEA multivariate time-series format (9 colon-separated series, then class label).
Optional formats:
  py export_optional_formats.py EXPORT_FOLDER/dataset.npz --torch
  py export_optional_formats.py EXPORT_FOLDER/dataset.npz --hdf5
These require torch/h5py only when requested. They are not needed for canonical capture/export.
example_load_dataset.py demonstrates layouts without imposing a model architecture.

TIME ALIGNMENT AND QUALITY
Actual acquisition timestamps remain in canonical CSV. Export resamples to a regular 20 ms grid.
Only clips with complete counts, consecutive sequences, valid IMU, no rail readings,
no range clipping and no new queue drops are exported. At most a 19 ms edge hold is used;
internal missing sensor data is not silently interpolated into training samples.
Cue timings are guidance. Do not pretend the fixed 1-second cue is the exact gesture onset.
Full raw six-second clips permit cropping around human-reviewed onset, release and end markers.
The three-second action tensor is convenient for a starting model; inspect that the whole action/release lies inside it.
A release candidate is NOT ground truth; annotations.source identifies human-reviewed boundaries.
Bad/raw attempts remain available for troubleshooting but are excluded from learning.
Warnings need signal_reviewed=true; hard-quality failures cannot be overridden by user acceptance.

THROWS
A simulated ball grip is less bent than a full fist. No threshold crossing is required for release.
Learn continuous bend trajectories and their timing relative to acceleration/angular velocity.
Keep overhand and underhand as separate classes. Include swing-without-release and fist-only negatives.
Capture one throw plus natural finger opening per clip, with pre-grip and post-release context.
Review confusing examples before deciding final window length or event-alignment method.

EVALUATION AND PREPROCESSING
All windows/crops from the same clip MUST stay together. Prefer holding out whole wear sessions.
Personal model: reserve unseen re-wear sessions for validation and final test.
Default split roles are unassigned; no optimistic random frame-level split is created.
Fit any global scaler/normaliser on TRAIN only, then apply unchanged to validation/test.
Per-wear open/fist/gyro calibration is a separate, explicit procedure that can also be performed at deployment.
If training with X_session_calibrated, reproduce the same calibration at runtime. Do not train calibrated and infer on raw values.
If using raw features, account for fit drift through varied wear sessions and train-only scaling.
Range/filter/feature-order changes require consistent acquisition and inference preprocessing, or a new dataset version.
Report held-out per-class precision/recall/confusion, especially the two throws and background false positives.
Twenty to thirty clean TRAIN clips per gesture is a starter target, not a promise of sufficient data.
