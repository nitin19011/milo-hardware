Milo personal glove training data: three flex sensors
Feature order: ax, ay, az, gx, gy, gz, thumb, index, middle.
50 Hz; full clip 2 seconds / 100 frames; N x 100 x 9 time-major or N x 9 x 100 channel-major.
10 human-confirmed clean examples of each of 7 actions; sequential class blocks. No automatic semantic validation.
Classes: come, sit, paw, spin, bang, throw_overhand, throw_underhand. Both throws prompt Milo fetch.
The first second is preparation; the second includes motion and follow-through. Throws include loose grip and finger release.
Raw flex ADC counts and IMU physical units (m/s^2, rad/s) are retained. Per-wear normalisation uses open/fist medians.
Flex normalisation is continuous and not clipped, not BENT/NOT_BENT; binary thresholds are diagnostic only.
calibration.json and calibration_report.txt contain all three fingers' baseline, span, noise and trial thresholds.
Class folders contain accepted CSV/JSON pairs; manifest.csv maps labels to files.
export/dataset.npz has raw/calibrated arrays, channel-major alternatives, integer/string labels and session grouping.
CSV, JSONL, NPY and .ts exports are included; export_optional_formats.py can make PyTorch/HDF5 if installed.
These are personal positive TRAIN examples only, with no independent validation/test or background/negative examples.
Group by clip/wear session for any later split; never split overlapping windows from one clip across train/evaluation.
Only glove sensors were captured. Spoken phrases were prompts, not recorded audio or measured alignment.
Release candidates are heuristic review aids, not ground truth timestamps. Review annotations remain explicitly unannotated.
Do not merge with previous thumb/index-only sessions: those have no measured middle channel.
Motor remains off throughout this firmware, including startup.
